Bottom line for the owner. On the same profiles, Florida Sales is cheaper on 8 of the 9 sizes both vendors quote. The gaps are widest on the mid/large beams — 3x9/2x9 ($2.26/ft), 3x5/2x5 ($1.76/ft), 3x6/2x6 ($1.66/ft) and 3x7/2x7 ($1.58/ft) are all cheaper at Florida. The only item where RMC (Hidro Paking) beats Florida is the 1x2 Open Back ($1.20 vs $1.28/ft). Note: Florida does not carry the 3x11/2x11 SMB, so RMC is the only source there ($12.28/ft). RMC figures are the per-piece cost divided by length, which is consistent across the 20-ft and 24-ft bars.
